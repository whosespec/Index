---
name: explore
description: How to research a codebase quickly and report findings. Use for read-only investigation.
---
1. Start broad (`ls`, Glob, Grep for names/keywords), then narrow to the specific files.
2. Read only what's needed; prefer targeted line ranges.
3. Report: direct answer first, then evidence as `path:line` bullets, then open questions.
4. Distinguish facts you verified from assumptions. Never invent files or APIs.
